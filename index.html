Agricultural plastics in the Metapontino

Greenhouse and tunnel covers detected from Sentinel-2, by municipality

—
—

Greenhouse and tunnel covers were detected with the Advanced Plastic Greenhouse Index applied to Sentinel-2 winter composites (Dec–Feb), at a locally calibrated threshold of 0.23. Both epochs were reprocessed together in September 2026.

Accuracy: user’s accuracy 84.2% for 2024, from 37 reference points interpreted on high-resolution imagery; Cohen’s kappa 0.849 for 2018, from 147 independent test points.

Density is greenhouse-covered area as a share of the agricultural land analysed — CLC classes 211–243 from the regional land use map, clipped to the Sentinel-2 study extent. This is smaller than the full municipal territory.

Ongoing research. DAFE, University of Basilicata.
